FAQ

Where are my records saved?
Only on your device. They are never sent to our servers.
My records are gone. Can I get them back?
We can't recover records lost because of a broken device, deleted data and so on. If you exported a backup file earlier, restore it from Menu > Backup > Restore in the app.
How do I make a backup?
Tap Menu > Backup > Export to a file, then choose where to save it. Save it outside the app, for example in iCloud Drive, because files inside the app are deleted along with the app. We recommend backing up about once a month. The file contains your records and images as they are, so keep it somewhere private.
I'm getting a new iPhone. What should I do?
Export a backup on the old device, send the file to the new one with AirDrop, iCloud Drive, email or similar, and restore it there. Keep the records on the old device until you've confirmed everything moved over.
Which time zone and currency does the app use?
The first time you open it, the app uses your iPhone's time zone and the currency of your region. You can change both in Menu > Time zone & currency. Market sessions (Tokyo, London, New York) are worked out for your time zone, including daylight saving time. Changing the setting doesn't move past records, and amounts are not converted.
What plans are there?
A free plan, Plus (monthly), and Pro (monthly or yearly). Prices for your country are shown in the app and on the App Store.
Every feature is open for 14 days from the day you first open the app. The trial is counted inside the app: no purchase or payment method is needed, and it never turns into a paid plan on its own. When it ends, you move to the free plan. If you subscribe during the trial, billing starts that day and the remaining trial days don't carry over.
As a launch offer, Pro (yearly) may be available at a discounted price for the first year, for people who haven't used a first-year price before. It renews at the regular yearly price after that. The offer may end without notice.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Go to Settings > your name > Subscriptions on your device. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Canceling doesn't delete your records; you'll see what the free plan shows.
My purchase isn't showing.
Tap Restore purchases on the Plan screen in the app.
I'd like a refund.
Apple handles purchases. Refunds follow Apple's procedures and applicable law. To request one, use Apple's page at reportaproblem.apple.com.
