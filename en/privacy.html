Playbook Trading Journal Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026 · Contact: fxrulenote@gmail.com

1. In short

Playbook Trading Journal (the "App") does not send your trade records to us. All of your records are saved only on your device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics.

2. Information the App handles

InformationWhere it is kept / Do we collect it?
Playbooks, trades, tags, chart images, settings (time zone, currency, colors, pair order and so on)Only on your device / No
Backup files you exportWherever you choose to save them / No
Safety copy (a precautionary copy of your records and images in the App's own storage)On your device, in the App's own storage / No
Subscription status (your plan)Managed by Apple / We only check whether you have a subscription (see section 4)

3. Information the App does not collect

The App does not automatically collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers (such as advertising IDs), usage data or crash reports. If we ever add anything that collects such information, we will update this policy first.

If you have turned on sharing analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us crash logs and aggregated usage statistics under Apple's own policies. These never include your trade records.

4. Purchases

Apple processes purchases, renewals, cancellations and refunds for paid plans. We never receive your credit card or other payment details. To check your subscription status, the App receives subscription information from Apple (StoreKit) on your device. Apple handles your payment information under Apple's own privacy policy.

5. When you email us

Only if you contact us by email do we use your email address and message, and only to reply and to help you. We use Google's Gmail service to send, receive and store email. We do not use them for anything else.

6. Advertising, tracking and sharing

The App has no advertising and no analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites. We do not sell your personal information or provide it to third parties, except where we are required to disclose it by law (for example, a lawful request from a court or a government authority).

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect access logs (such as IP addresses) when you visit these pages; we do not use them.

7. Backup files

A backup file contains your trade records and chart images as they are. You are responsible for how you store or share an exported file (cloud storage, email, messages and so on). We recommend saving it where others can't see it.

8. Deleting your records

  • Deleting the App, deleting its data in the App, or resetting your device deletes the records on your device. We cannot recover them.
  • Your records may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), and restoring such a backup may bring them back. You manage your device backups; we have no access to them.
  • Because we don't hold your records, there is nothing for us to delete, and you don't need to ask us to delete them.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information a business holds about you. Since we hold no personal information from the App, there is normally nothing to provide or delete. If you have emailed us and want that email deleted, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Children

The App is a tool for recording trading activity and is meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you are a minor, please use the App only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page, in the App, or on the App Store.
